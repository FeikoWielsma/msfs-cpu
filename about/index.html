What the guide measures

The benchmark explorer combines published MSFS 2024 results from Tom's Hardware, PC Games Hardware and ComputerBase. I compile their results and make the combined analysis and charts here; I have not personally benchmarked every part.

Different reviewers use different scenes and settings, so raw FPS cannot be compared across all tests. The Performance Index aligns overlapping datasets onto a relative scale. CPU and GPU indices are separate; GPU resolutions are also separate. An index is not a promise of FPS in your sim.

Open Sources & methodology on the benchmark page for the detailed approach and links to the original reviews. Please visit those publications for their testing and context.

The build guide adds my judgement about balanced parts and budgets. Its combined build score ranks suggestions; it is not a measured whole-system benchmark. Derived scores are marked, and price dates and estimates are shown on the guide. Check the retailer's current price, exact model, availability and compatibility before buying.

The MainThread and GPU illustration explains bottlenecks using a simplified model. Its FPS figures are illustrative and should not be used as purchasing evidence.

Policy

The tools, charts and recommendations are free to use. If you find them helpful, you can leave an optional tip on Ko-fi to support the time I spend maintaining the project. A tip does not buy a recommendation or priority support.

There are currently no retailer affiliate links on this site. I am exploring affiliate programs as another way to fund maintenance. This page will be updated when any are introduced.

If affiliate links are added, they will be labelled near the shopping links, and I may earn a commission on qualifying purchases. Recommendations and rankings will remain independent of commission. A shop will not need an affiliate program to be worth mentioning, and payment will not buy a higher position in the benchmarks.

Build estimates are reference prices, not retailer offers or a guarantee that a particular shop can supply the whole build at the displayed total.
