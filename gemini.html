1. What is a "CPU Bottleneck"?

Before any pixel can be rendered, your CPU must calculate aerodynamics, aircraft avionics (glass cockpit screens), runway logic, and issue instructions called Draw Calls to the GPU.

If the CPU takes 22ms to finish that math, you will get at most 45 FPS, regardless of whether you have an RX 9070 or an RTX 4090.

2. What is a "GPU Bottleneck"?

The GPU is the artist. It turns draw calls into actual colored pixels on screen, calculating 3D volumetric light through clouds, reflections on airplane wings, and anti-aliasing.

Up in the clouds at 35,000 ft, the ground is simple, so the CPU works with ease. Here the GPU runs at 99% - 100% capacity, meaning a 12% faster GPU yields almost exactly 12% higher FPS.

3. How to fix each in MSFS?
If MainThread Limited: Lower Terrain Level of Detail (TLOD), decrease Airport Traffic / Ground Vehicles, or pick an AMD CPU with 3D V-Cache (e.g. 9800X3D).
If GPU Limited: Enable FSR / DLSS Super Resolution, lower Volumetric Clouds from Ultra to High, or step down resolution.
