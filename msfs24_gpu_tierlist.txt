⛔ **8 GB is the trap.** Same GPU, 16 GB vs 8 GB, measured here: **+59%** at 1440p and **+120%** at 4K. A 5060 Ti 16GB beats the 8GB version by more than the 8GB version beats a 4060. Every `*` card above is a VRAM cliff waiting to happen.

**TL;DR**
• VRAM > everything at these settings. Don't buy 8 GB for MSFS, at any price.
• Resolution decides how much the GPU matters: the field spans 5.5x at 1080p but **17x** at 4K. MSFS is CPU-bound at 1080p, so a big GPU there is wasted without a big CPU.
• Best value in B: 9060XT 16GB matches a 5070 and a 7800 XT.
• AMD is competitive up high — 9070 XT and 7900 XTX both land within 6% of a 4090.
• Below ~25 the ordering is mush: those cards are all VRAM-starved and their exact places are noise, not ranking.

Caveats: one source (Tom's Hardware), most cards from a single test pass, and the index is normalised **per resolution** — 1440p numbers aren't comparable to 4K ones.
