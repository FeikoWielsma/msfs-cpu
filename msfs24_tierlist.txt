Ranges are fastest-to-slowest SKU in the group, so a wide one (Core 13/14th) means the flagship and the i3 are far apart.

⛔ **Don't buy the 16 MB L3 Cezanne traps** — 5500, 5700 (non-X!), 5600G, 5700G. Never benchmarked, so no score, but they carry **half** the 5600's cache (16 vs 32 MB) plus PCIe 3.0. Cache is worth +10-20% here, so they land at or below the 3600 (47).
⚠️ **"5700" and "5700X" are different chips!** 5700X = Vermeer, 32 MB, fine-ish (C tier). 5700 = Cezanne, 16 MB, avoid.

**TL;DR**
• V-Cache is worth **+19-30%** over the same generation without it — a full gen of uplift, or more.
• Arrow Lake, Raptor Lake and Zen 4 X3D flagships land within **1%** of each other. That fight's a wash.
• Cores do nothing: the 16-core 5950X scores *below* the 8-core 5800X. Buy cache, not core count.
• DDR4 costs Alder Lake **~7%** (measured, 12700K on both) — get the DDR5 board if you can.
• On AM4? V-Cache, any of it: 5800X3D `69` > 5700X3D `66` > 5600X3D `61`. (5500X3D untested, but same 96 MB die.) Nothing else on the socket is worth the money.
