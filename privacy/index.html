Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

You can use the tools without an account. This page explains the data involved in visiting the site, contacting me, or leaving an optional tip.

Who is responsible?

Feiko Wielsma, known as Razortek, operates msfs.razortek.nl as an independent personal project in the Netherlands and is the controller for the processing described here. Contact msfs@razortek.nl about this policy or your data.

Microsoft, Asobo and the official MSFS Discord do not operate this site.

Visiting the site

GitHub Pages hosts the website and serves its pages, images, fonts and data files. Your browser sends connection information, including your IP address and browser information, when it requests those files. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

Delivering and protecting the site serves the legitimate interests of making this free resource available and preventing abuse (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). GitHub also processes service data for its own purposes under its privacy statement, which describes its retention and international-transfer arrangements.

The fonts are served from this site's own files. Your browser does not need to contact Google Fonts to display the pages.

Browser preferences & build selections

The site stores your light or dark theme preference in local storage under msfs-theme. If you disable analytics below, it stores your choice under umami.disabled. These preferences stay in this browser until removed or changed; they are not site accounts or advertising identifiers.

The tools calculate results in your browser. Build selections can appear in the query string and resolution in the URL fragment, so a link can reproduce a selection. Anyone you share that link with can see those selections. The hosting service receives the requested path and query string; URL fragments are not sent in the HTTP request.

You can remove stored preferences through your browser's site-data settings. The site uses these settings to provide the display and privacy choices you request.

Website analytics

The three tool pages use Umami at stats.feikowielsma.nl, privately self-hosted in the Netherlands. The About page and this privacy page do not load analytics.

Analytics records page visits, referring pages, visit times, screen size, browser, operating system, device category, language and approximate location. The analytics endpoint receives your IP address and user agent as part of the connection; Umami uses connection information to derive location and visit statistics. Its session records do not store a raw IP-address field. This site's integration does not supply a name, email address, account identifier, form contents, session recording or heatmap data.

The tracker does not use analytics cookies. It is configured to omit query strings and URL fragments from recorded page URLs, respect the browser's Do Not Track setting, and record visits only on msfs.razortek.nl. The aim is to understand which resources people use and improve them, relying on legitimate interests for limited website statistics (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). You may object or opt out below.

Retention: no automatic expiry is currently configured for the analytics database. Historical statistics remain until manually deleted. The analytics server's reverse-proxy access logs can contain IP addresses and requested URLs; those logs are configured to rotate with a maximum age of 14 days, with size limits that may shorten this. Docker's separate service-output logs rotate by size, without a fixed age limit.

The server configuration includes local backups and a Google Drive backup copy with 28-day retention. Backups may contain analytics records and logs, so deleting live records does not immediately remove all backup copies. Google may process backup data outside the Netherlands under its service arrangements.

Checking this browser's analytics preference…

The choice applies to this browser and site origin. It does not remove existing records or disable the hosting provider's necessary connection logs. Do Not Track, if enabled in your browser, is respected independently of this stored choice.

Email & community messages

If you email msfs@razortek.nl, I receive your email address, message, attachments and mail-routing information. Mailgun receives the email and forwards it to my Gmail inbox. I use it to answer questions, investigate corrections and handle privacy or project enquiries, based on the legitimate interest of responding to people who contact me (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Privacy-rights requests may also be processed to meet legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

Correspondence is kept while the enquiry and necessary follow-up remain open. A relevant record may be kept longer where needed to explain a correction, resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation; unrelated personal details are not needed for a public correction. I do not add correspondents to a marketing list.

Mailgun and Google process email through their services, which can involve processing outside the EEA. See the Mailgun privacy policy and Google privacy policy for their service, retention and transfer information.

If you contact me through Discord instead, Discord handles the message under its privacy policy. Public channel messages are visible to other community members. Do not post sensitive details there.

Optional tips on Ko-fi

The Ko-fi link takes you to a separate website. No Ko-fi payment widget or payment form is embedded here. Ko-fi and its payment services handle checkout; I do not receive your full card details from this site.

As the creator, I may receive the supporter name, email address, message, amount, transaction reference and other details shared through Ko-fi or the payment provider. I use those details to administer the tip, handle a payment question or refund, and keep necessary records. The bases are legitimate interests in administering voluntary support (Article 6(1)(f)) and applicable recordkeeping obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

Payment records are kept for the applicable statutory retention period where one applies, or while needed for payment administration and disputes. A tip is not consent to marketing. Ko-fi explains its own processing and international arrangements in its privacy policy; the payment provider's policy also applies to its processing.

Other links, sharing & international processing

There are currently no retailer affiliate links, advertising pixels or embedded retailer widgets on the site. If that changes, this policy and the disclosures next to shopping links will be updated before those integrations are introduced.

Following a source, Discord, Ko-fi or retailer link opens a service with its own privacy practices. The destination receives connection information when you visit it. Those services do not receive a request merely because their text link is displayed here.

The service providers named above receive data as needed to host the site, run analytics, back up the server, deliver email or process a tip. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to establish or defend a legal claim. I do not sell visitor or correspondent data or use it to build advertising profiles.

GitHub, Google, Mailgun, Ko-fi and payment providers may process information outside the EEA. Depending on the service and recipient, safeguards can include an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. Their linked privacy statements explain the safeguards they use and how to request more information.

Your rights & questions

Depending on the processing and applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. If processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it. These rights are subject to legal conditions, including records that must be retained.

Email msfs@razortek.nl to exercise a right or raise a concern. I aim to respond within one month; where a lawful extension is needed, I will explain it. I may request proportionate information to verify your request. Because the tools have no visitor accounts and analytics does not use an account identity, I may not be able to link a statistical record to you without additional information.

The build generator makes hardware suggestions, not automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about people. There is no requirement to send personal information to use the tools; without contact details I may be unable to answer an enquiry.

You can complain to the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens or your local data-protection authority. You do not have to contact me first.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when the site's data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be explained here.
