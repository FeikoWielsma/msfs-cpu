⛔ Do not buy — the 16 MB L3 Cezanne traps

5500 · 5700 (non-X!) · 5600G · 5700G — half the 5600's cache, so ≤ a 3600. The 5700X is a different chip (32 MB, C tier).

Ryzen 5 5500 · Ryzen 7 5700 (non-X) · 5600G · 5700G

None of these appear in any benchmark set, so they get no score — but they carry half the 5600's cache (16 vs 32 MB) plus PCIe 3.0. Cache is the single strongest factor measured here (32 → 96 MB is worth +10–20%), so they land at or below the Ryzen 5 3600.

⚠ “5700” and “5700X” are different chips. The 5700X is Vermeer with 32 MB and lands in C tier. The plain 5700 is Cezanne with 16 MB — avoid it.

What the data says

V-Cache is worth +19–30% over the same generation without it — a full generation of uplift, or more.
Arrow Lake, Raptor Lake and Zen 4 X3D flagships land within 1% of each other. That fight is a wash.
Cores do nothing. The 16-core 5950X scores below the 8-core 5800X. Buy cache, not core count.
DDR4 costs Alder Lake ~7% — measured on a 12700K run on both. Enough to drop it a whole tier.
On AM4? V-Cache, any of it: 5800X3D 69 › 5700X3D 66 › 5600X3D 61. Nothing else on the socket is worth the money.
Absolute FPS are not comparable across sites — different scenes and settings. That is exactly why the index exists.
