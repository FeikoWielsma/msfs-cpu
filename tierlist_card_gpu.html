⛔ 8 GB is the trap — not the GPU, the VRAM

Same GPU, 16 GB vs 8 GB, measured here: at . Every 8G card above is a cliff waiting to happen.

The same GPU with double the memory is worth at this resolution — measured on three matched pairs (5060 Ti, 9060 XT and 4060 Ti, 16 GB vs 8 GB). Across the three resolutions that penalty runs +29% at 1080p, +59% at 1440p and +120% at 4K: the higher you go, the more the memory decides it rather than the chip.

⚠ Small gaps are ties — one source, and 24 of 35 cards come from a single test pass.

What the data says

VRAM beats everything at these settings. Do not buy an 8 GB card for MSFS 2024 at any price.
At this resolution the field spans from the fastest card to the slowest.
Resolution decides how much the GPU matters at all: the spread runs 5.5× at 1080p, 8.7× at 1440p and 17× at 4K.
AMD is competitive up high — the 9070 XT and 7900 XTX sit alongside the 4090 and 5080 rather than behind them.
Tier letters mean the same thing on all three cards: a fixed share of the fastest card, so S at 4K is far rarer than S at 1080p.
