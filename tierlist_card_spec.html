Three hardware tiers for MSFS 2024, each pick carrying its measured Performance Index so you can see how far apart the tiers actually are. CPU, GPU and VRAM rows come from benchmark data; RAM is not something this dataset measures.

Where the money actually goes

VRAM before the chip. The same GPU with 16 GB instead of 8 GB measures +59% at 1440p and +120% at 4K on three matched pairs. That is why 16 GB is the floor at every tier here, including minimum — an 8 GB card falls off a cliff rather than degrading gently.

Cache before cores on the CPU side. V-Cache is worth +19–30% over the same generation without it, and a 16-core 5950X scores below an 8-core 5800X. It is also why the AMD column climbs further: 98 against a ceiling of 79 on the Intel side, where there is no X3D equivalent.

Not measured here: RAM, storage, bandwidth and OS follow the official requirements — Windows 10 with the latest update · DirectX 12 · 50 GB storage · 10 / 50 / 100 Mbps.
Measured rows: CPU index from seven Tom's Hardware, PCGH and ComputerBase test passes; GPU index at 1440p Ultra from Tom's Hardware, 5090 = 100. Both are relative scales, not frame rates — absolute FPS are not comparable across sites. ★ marks a value pick.
